# MoaV Deploy — Privacy Policy

MoaV Deploy is a mobile app for administering a MoaV server that you own, over SSH. This policy describes how the app handles data.

## Data we collect

None. We operate no backend and no account system. The app does not send your data to us or to any third party.

## On-device data

SSH keys, hostnames, and connection settings you enter are stored only in your device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android) so the app can reconnect. They never leave your device except as part of the SSH connection you initiate to your own server. Deleting the app removes them.

## Tracking and advertising

No analytics, no tracking, and no advertising identifiers.

## Your server

Anything you do on your server through this app is governed by your own arrangements with your hosting provider and your users, not by us.
